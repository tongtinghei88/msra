### RA 01 — 屈鐵風險評估 (Bar Bending / Rebar Processing)

**Process Name:** Bar Banding (屈鐵)  
**Location:** Both on-site and off-site bending yard  
**Affected Workers:** Bar bending workers, Bar bending machine operators  
**Date:** 08-04-2020 | Version: 0

#### Work Steps & Hazards:

| # | Circumstance (Step) | Hazard | Related Party | L | S | Risk | Priority |
|---|---------------------|--------|--------------|---|---|------|----------|
| 1.1 | Bar bending (both on site & off site) | Twist injury from incorrect manual handling | Bar bending workers | 3 | 1 | L | 3 |
| 1.2 | Bar bending | Slash injury from protruding rebars | Bar bending workers | 3 | 1 | L | 3 |
| 1.3 | Bar bending | Slippage / tipping | Bar bending workers | 3 | 1 | L | 3 |
| 1.4 | Bar bending | Hands & fingers struck by moving parts of bar bending machine / moving rebars | Bar bending machine operators | 3 | 2 | H | 2 |
| 1.5 | Bar bending | Injury caused by unauthorized entry | Person entering fence off area unauthorized | 3 | 2 | H | 2 |
| 1.6 | Bar bending | Electric shock | Bar bending machine operators | 2 | 3 | VH | 1 |

#### Control Measures:

**1.1 Twist injury — Manual Handling**
- Existing: Establish fixed routing, Give break for workers at fixed period, Workers cannot maintain mental focus are not allowed to perform this task
- Additional: For large diameter rebar (Y32-Y50) requiring multi-person manual handling, one person should be responsible for synchronizing movement of workers. Conduct pre-work exercise. Promotion of Safe working cycle.
- PPE: Safety Helmet with chin strap, Reflective jacket, Safety Shoes
- Responsible: Project manager, Site Agent, Safety personnel, Safety Committee member
- Training: Manual handling
- Residual: L=1, S=2

**1.2 Slash injury from protruding rebars**
- Existing: All protruding rebar tips to be properly covered, Maintain good house keeping, Establish safe access & egress
- Additional: Establish fixed routing, Steel wires used to tie up rebars to be cleaned up daily
- PPE: Safety Helmet with chin strap, Reflective jacket, Safety Shoes
- Training: Housekeeping
- Residual: L=2, S=1

**1.3 Slippage / tipping**
- Existing: Establish safe access & egress, Maintain good housekeeping
- Additional: Stagnant water and debris to be cleaned, Wear safety shoes w/ sufficient treads
- PPE: Safety Helmet with chin strap, Reflective jacket, Safety Shoes
- Training: Housekeeping
- Residual: L=2, S=1

**1.4 Hands & fingers struck by machine**
- Existing: Only skilled workers authorized by sub-contractor are allowed to operate bar bending machine, No unauthorized entry, Bar bending machine equipped w/ emergency stop, Leather gloves worn at all times
- Additional: Operators who are mentally unfocused or sick are not allowed to work, Flashlight to be installed to warn operators, Workers to pay attention hand/finger position, Reinforced inspection for off-site bending yard
- PPE: Safety Helmet with chin strap, Reflective jacket, Safety Shoes
- Training: In house rules for rebar bending
- Residual: L=2, S=2

**1.5 Unauthorized entry**
- Existing: Assembly area to be fenced-off authorized by competent person, "Unauthorized Entry prohibited" sign displayed in prominent places, Work to be stopped upon unauthorized entry is found
- Additional: Site personnel to confirm no other work to be performed within fence-off area
- PPE: Safety Helmet with chin strap, Reflective jacket, Safety Shoes
- Training: Method statement
- Residual: L=2, S=2 → **Actually L=1, S=2 in doc**

**1.6 Electric shock**
- Existing: Bar bending machine must be repaired by Registered electrician, Insulated gloves and safety shoes worn at all times
- Additional: Electric cables to be hung up or protected underground by robust material, Workers should not wear damaged safety shoes
- Training: Safe use of electricity
- Residual: L=1, S=3

#### Key Control Measures by Section:

**Section A — Bamboo Scaffolding:**
- A.1: Follow latest edition COP for bamboo scaffolding safety & CIC guideline on planking arrangements. Provide ingress & egress. Inspect by competent person (min 10 years experience) with Form 5 signed. No green bamboo. Pre-install independent life line. Provide proper fencing & toe board. Do not modify scaffold.
  - PPE: Safety Helmet w/ chin strap, Reflective vest, Safety Shoes, Full body harness w/ fall arrestor, Leather Gloves, Long sleeve clothing, Arc welding goggles
  - Training: Labour Department COP for bamboo scaffolding safety, CIC guideline
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- A.2: All hand-held tools equipped with lanyards. Small tools carried in suitable containers. No throwing of objects. Operation zone fenced-off with warning signs. Containers for lifting must have valid certificate.
  - Residual: L=1, S=2

- A.3: Cover all bamboo scaffolding with fire blanket. Enforce Hot work permit. Remove combustible objects. Powder fire extinguisher at operation area. Spray water at nearby area. Post-work fire inspection.
  - PPE: Full body harness w/ double lanyard, Leather Gloves, Long sleeve clothing
  - Training: Prevention of fire
  - Residual: L=2, S=1

**Section B — Arc Welding:**
- B.1: Enforce Hot work permit. Fence-off operation area with warning signs. Remove combustible objects. Powder fire extinguisher at operation area. Spray water. Post-work inspection.
  - Residual: L=1, S=2

- B.2: Workers wear long sleeve clothing, leather gloves and welding mask. Use flame-resistant plating to shield sparks (especially near Logos Academy).
  - Residual: L=2, S=1

- B.3: Provide blower fan to ventilate toxic fumes. Provide N95 mask.
  - Residual: L=2, S=1

- B.4: Use protective eyewear (polarized glasses). Set up rest time (5 mins/hour). Stop work if eye discomfort. Shield off area with non-reflective barrier.
  - Residual: L=2, S=1

**Section C — Transportation:**
- C.1: Establish fixed routing. Give periodic breaks. Use trolleys to minimize manual handling. Mentally unfocused workers not allowed. For multi-person handling, one person synchronizes movement. Pre-work exercise. Identify which ELS can be carried manually.
  - Residual: L=1, S=2

**Section D — Flame Cutting:**
- D.1: Long sleeve clothing, leather gloves, flame cutting mask. Flame-resistant plating for spark shielding (especially near Logos Academy).
  - Residual: L=2, S=1 → (RA03 .doc has L=1, S=2)

- D.2: Provide blower fan. Provide N95 mask.
  - Residual: L=2, S=1 → (RA03 .doc has L=1, S=2)

- D.3: Fence-off area with warning signs. Flame cutting set with flashback arrestor, non-return valve, pressure regulator with gauges. Daily gas leak check with soap water. Replace worn/damaged gas hoses. Cylinders on trolley/cages isolated from heat source. Safety enforces hot work permit. Only certified workers appointed by safety department. Valve keys not left unattended. Cover cylinders with canvas to avoid direct sunlight.
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- D.4: Protective eyewear (polarized glasses). Rest time 5 mins/hour. Stop work if eye discomfort. Shield area with non-reflective paint.
  - Residual: L=2, S=1

**Section E — Tower Crane Lifting:**
- E.1: No over-capacity lifting. Don't tamper safety devices. Clear lifting route. No lifting during heavy rain/typhoon #3+. Check load chart. Check crane in safe working order. Stop if abnormalities. Don't use crane for pulling.
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- E.2: All LG with valid certificate. Color code system. Daily checking. Proper rigging method. Discard damaged LG (webbing sling & steel wires). Ensure ELS fully disengaged before lifting.
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- E.3: Tower crane & operator with valid certificate. Operator daily check (LA/LG Form 1 + in-house checklist). No over-capacity. Don't tamper safety devices. Clear lifting path. No pulling. Fence-off lifting zone with warning signs. Ensure ELS fully disengaged.
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- E.4: Fence-off with warning signs. Stop work upon unauthorized entry. Site personnel confirm no other works in zone. Foreman watches for unauthorized entry.
  - Residual: L=1, S=3

- E.5: Use double-end guide rope. Don't use crane for pulling. Workers must not stand in path of suspended load. No lifting during heavy rain/typhoon #3+. All personnel with valid certificates. No sudden crane movement. Mark "dead zones" and inform workers at pre-work briefing. Blow whistle before lifting. Place solid wooden blocks at unloading area.

DO NOT walk or sit on I-beam.
